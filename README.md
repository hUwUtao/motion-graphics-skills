# Video Production Skills for LLM agents

A pack of reusable instruction spanning on the topic of making a video with scripted tools. Not limit to any workflow, and there is a tool being developed for it.

## Installation

```
npx skills add https://github.com/huwutao/motion-graphics-skills
```

## Why?

AI are making videos, yes! Not the "predict what look best", the frontier LLM are now making informal video, by recipe. However, we can easily spot it out for anyone has a taste. This will not the way of doing that, its not a skill that you can just attach and let the AI tell a story. The story is yours. As far as i observe, they can't think of anything original.

Though I might considered giving myself to slop, I still want to have some identity of...me, or rather arts. This guarantee for the least wrong assumption, you can be a part of the production, not leaving it. You will not be limited with a crippled browser that screenshots a lot of frames, nor some crooked way an agent clicking around on your computer.

This is the premise to it.

## How?

While i'm making a thing that can potentially, make most of those video (except the realisty). A bridge, where LLM can make it home to its mocking, storyboarding, rendering, simulating in what we can comprehend.

Whats the approach?

Video are data. Don't limit it to the generation...

Treat the project as also...a bunch of data. The goal is not to make it reproducible with any parameter, the goal is to make it a mental writing. You won't write a poem with a logical function.

## Whats included?

Too much words for a thing. This guide will joint with a tool that capable, in order to bring a tolerable progress being done in your video task. It can break into those sorts of sub-skills:

- Video editing, compositing
- 2D/3D motion graphics design
- Production values: aesthetics, quality and data streamlineness
- and few more
