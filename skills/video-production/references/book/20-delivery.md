## 20. Delivery

### 20.1 Codecs, Bitrates, Frame Rates, Resolutions, Aspect Ratios, and Color Metadata Tags

Definition: The compression architecture, chroma subsampling, bit depth, GOP structure, bitrate allocation, and container color-space signaling (`NCLC` / `VUI` atoms) used to encode rendered frames into archival masters and distribution deliverables ([ITU-R BT.709](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2Fr-rec-bt.709); [ITU-R BT.2100](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2Fr-rec-bt.2100); [YouTube Encoding Spec](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en)).
Why:

- Emotion: Sufficient bitrate and 10-bit precision preserve subtle atmospheric gradients, film grain, and dark shadow nuance so the emotional mood survives distribution.
- Attention: Prevents macroblock breakup during high-velocity particle bursts or whip transitions from distracting the viewer's eye.
- Production value: Explicit NCLC color tagging (`1-1-1` for SDR BT.709) prevents the dreaded washed-out gamma shift across browsers and operating systems.
- Clarity: High-profile CABAC encoding with closed GOPs ensures razor-sharp typographic edges across every frame.
- Cost: Two-tier delivery (10-bit/12-bit mezzanine master + target-bitrate distribution encodes) avoids ever having to re-render the 3D/2D scene for new platforms.
  Rules:

1. **Two-Tier Encoding Architecture (Mezzanine Master vs. Distribution Deliverable)**:
   - **Tier 1 — Archival / Mezzanine Master**: Always render from the GPU pipeline first to an intra-frame mezzanine master (**10-bit 4:2:2 Intra-Frame Mezzanine (ProRes 422 HQ / DNxHR HQX)** `10-bit 4:2:2` at `~220 Mbps` for `1080p24` / `~880 Mbps` for `4K24`, or **ProRes 4444** `12-bit 4:4:4 + Alpha` when transparency or crisp red-on-black vector edges require uncompressed chroma) or `16-bit` OpenEXR image sequence ([Netflix Delivery Specs](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fstudiopartner.netflix.net%2Fstudio%2Fdelivery-specifications-and-requirements-external)).
   - **Tier 2 — Web / Social / Client Distribution (`.mp4` Container)**: Transcode from the Mezzanine Master using **2-Pass VBR** (with `max_bitrate = 1.5 × target_bitrate`) or **CRF `15–18`** (`H.264 / AVC High Profile`, `H.265 / HEVC Main10`, or `AV1`) with **progressive scan (never interlaced)**, **`Closed GOP` (`keyframe interval = fps / 2` or `1.0–2.0 seconds`)**, **`2` consecutive B-frames**, and **`CABAC` entropy coding** ([YouTube Upload Encoding Settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en)).
2. **The 4:2:0 Chroma Subsampling Typography Defense**:
   - All standard consumer web video (`H.264 8-bit 4:2:0`) discards **`75%` of color (Cb/Cr) resolution** (storing only 1 color sample per `2×2` block of luma pixels). If small high-chroma red or blue text (`stroke < 3 px`) is placed directly on a dark background with equal luminance ($\Delta Y \approx 0$), `4:2:0` subsampling smears the edges into unreadable staircase blocks.
   - **Rule**: Ensure all fine edges and small typography (`< 4% H`) carry **Luminance Contrast ($\Delta Y \ge 0.35$)** rather than pure chroma contrast, or keep stroke widths `≥ 2.5–3.0 px` on even pixel coordinates.
3. **Master Bitrate & Parameter Matrix**:

| Resolution & Frame Rate                                   | Aspect Ratio    | H.264 (AVC) Target Bitrate (SDR `8-bit`)                                                      | H.265 (HEVC `10-bit`) / AV1 Target Bitrate                           | Audio Stream Codec & Bitrate                     |
| :-------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- |
| **`1080p` (`1920×1080` or `1080×1920`) @ `24/25/30 fps`** | `16:9` / `9:16` | **`12–20 Mbps`** (YouTube min `8–10 Mbps`; increase to `20–25 Mbps` if heavy grain/particles) | **`8–14 Mbps`** (`Main10` profile eliminates 8-bit gradient banding) | `AAC-LC`, `48 kHz`, `320 kbps` (or `≥ 256 kbps`) |
| **`1080p` @ `60 fps`**                                    | `16:9` / `9:16` | **`18–30 Mbps`** (YouTube min `12 Mbps`)                                                      | **`12–20 Mbps`**                                                     | `AAC-LC`, `48 kHz`, `320 kbps`                   |
| **`1440p` (`2560×1440`) @ `24/30 fps` (`60 fps`)**        | `16:9`          | **`24–32 Mbps`** (`36–48 Mbps` @ `60 fps`)                                                    | **`16–24 Mbps`** (`24–32 Mbps` @ `60 fps`)                           | `AAC-LC`, `48 kHz`, `320 kbps`                   |
| **`4K UHD` (`3840×2160`) @ `24/25/30 fps`**               | `16:9`          | **`40–65 Mbps`** (YouTube rec `35–45 Mbps` SDR, `44–56 Mbps` HDR)                             | **`25–45 Mbps`** (`Main10` mandatory for HDR10/HLG)                  | `AAC-LC`, `48 kHz`, `320–512 kbps`               |
| **`4K UHD` @ `60 fps`**                                   | `16:9`          | **`60–85 Mbps`** (YouTube rec `53–68 Mbps` SDR, `66–85 Mbps` HDR)                             | **`40–60 Mbps`**                                                     | `AAC-LC`, `48 kHz`, `320–512 kbps`               |

4. **Mandatory Container Metadata Signaling**:
   - Explicitly write `colr` / `VUI` flags into every `.mp4` / `.mov` header: for SDR HD/4K, set **`color_primaries = 1 (bt709)`, `color_trc = 1 (bt709)`, `colorspace = 1 (bt709)`, `color_range = tv / limited (16–235 in 8-bit, 64–940 in 10-bit)`** (never export unmarked `Full Range 0–255` YUV in H.264 unless a specialized pipeline explicitly requests it, as standard hardware video decoders map `16–235` to `0.0–1.0` and will crush the bottom/top `6%` of an unmarked Full Range stream into clipped black and white) ([ITU-R BT.709](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2Fr-rec-bt.709); [YouTube Encoding Settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en)).
   - Place the `.mp4` `moov` atom at the **front of the file (`+faststart`)** so web playback starts instantaneously without downloading the entire file.
     Event mapping: Final encoder specification `delivery_encode_config(container="mp4", faststart=true, video_codec="h264_high"|"hevc_main10", pixel_format="yuv420p"|"yuv420p10le", color_range="tv_limited", nclc=[1,1,1], gop_frames=12, b_frames=2, bitrate_mbps=20.0, audio_codec="aac_lc", audio_kbps=320)`.
     Raster/bake note: Convert final tone-mapped linear RGB `[0.0, 1.0]` to `BT.709` nonlinear `R'G'B'`, apply `±1.0 LSB` blue-noise dithering, transform via the exact `BT.709` luma-chroma matrix ($Y' = 0.2126 R' + 0.7152 G' + 0.0722 B'$), and scale to legal `16–235` (`8-bit`) or `64–940` (`10-bit`) code values before handing off to the hardware encoder.
     Cultural notes: PAL territories (Europe, UK, Australia, China, much of Asia) broadcast natively at **`25.00 fps` / `50.00 fps`**, whereas NTSC territories (North America, Japan, South Korea) broadcast at **`23.976` / `29.97` / `59.94 fps`** (or true integer **`24.00` / `30.00` / `60.00 fps`** for digital web/social delivery) ([SMPTE ST 12-1 Timecode](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fblog%2Funderstanding-standards-time-code)).
     Failure modes: Exporting H.264 with `Full Range (0–255)` while the player assumes `Legal/TV Range (16–235)` → all shadow detail below `IRE 6%` is crushed to solid black; omitting `+faststart` (`moov` atom at end) → web players stall for seconds before starting playback.
     Checklist: [ ] Mezzanine master archived in `10-bit` ProRes 422 HQ / `12-bit` 4444 or `16-bit` EXR [ ] Distribution `.mp4` uses Closed GOP (`≤ 1–2 s`), CABAC, progressive scan, and `+faststart` [ ] Bitrate scaled to resolution, frame rate, and grain/particle complexity [ ] Color metadata explicitly tagged (`NCLC 1-1-1`, `Legal/TV range 16–235 / 64–940`) [ ] Small typography carries luminance contrast ($\Delta Y \ge 0.35$) to survive `4:2:0` chroma subsampling
     Sources: [YouTube Recommended Upload Encoding Settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en); [ITU-R BT.709](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2Fr-rec-bt.709); [ITU-R BT.2100](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2Fr-rec-bt.2100); [SMPTE Timecode Standards](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fblog%2Funderstanding-standards-time-code); [Netflix Delivery Specifications](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fstudiopartner.netflix.net%2Fstudio%2Fdelivery-specifications-and-requirements-external).

### 20.2 Platform Delivery Specifications and SMPTE Title/Action Safe Margins

Definition: The platform-specific resolution, aspect ratio, frame-rate ceiling, bitrate re-compression behavior, and UI/overscan exclusion margins governing delivery to Netflix/Broadcast, YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram ([Netflix Partner Help](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fstudiopartner.netflix.net%2Fstudio%2Fdelivery-specifications-and-requirements-external); [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en); [TikTok Ads Help](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fads.tiktok.com%2Fresources%2Fhelp%2Farticle%2Ftiktok-auction-in-feed-ads%3Flang%3Den); [Meta Business Help](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2Fbusiness%2Fhelp%2F1038071743007909); [SMPTE ST 2046-1](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fhubfs%2FSMPTE%2520Standards%2520Quarterly%2520Report%2520September%25202024%2520%281%29.pdf%3FhsLang%3Den)).
Why:

- Emotion: Prevents awkward black pillar/letterbox bars or UI-obscured faces from breaking viewer immersion on the target device.
- Attention: Keeps focal action and CTAs inside the exact unobstructed viewport region of each platform.
- Production value: Uploading at `1080×1920` (`≤ 20 Mbps`) for TikTok/Reels avoids triggering aggressive platform server-side downscalers that crush `4K` uploads into blurry `720p` artifacts.
- Clarity: Ensures burned-in graphics, logos, and subtitles never collide with platform like/share icons or television bezel overscan.
- Cost: Following a unified Multi-Platform Safe-Margin Template allows automated multi-aspect crop/re-layout without manual rework.
  Rules:

2. **The Universal Vertical Safe Box (`9:16` Cross-Posting Rule)**:
   - When authoring a single `9:16` (`1080×1920`) vertical master destined for simultaneous distribution across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, constrain all essential typography, logos, and faces to the **Intersection Safe Box**:
     $$X \in [70\text{ px},\; 920\text{ px}] \quad (6.5\%\text{ to }85.2\%\text{ W}), \qquad Y \in [250\text{ px},\; 1420\text{ px}] \quad (13.0\%\text{ to }74.0\%\text{ H})$$
   - This single `850 × 1170 px` safe box simultaneously clears TikTok's right-hand engagement column, Reels' bottom caption drawer, Shorts' top status bar, AND stays 100% inside Instagram's `4:5` (`Y ∈ [285, 1635]`) profile grid crop!
     Event mapping: Viewport overlay validator `platform_safe_zone_validator(target_platforms=["smpte_2046", "tiktok", "reels", "shorts"], enforce_intersection_box=true, safe_x_norm=[0.065, 0.852], safe_y_norm=[0.130, 0.740])`.
     Raster/bake note: Render a toggleable semi-transparent diagnostic **Safe-Zone Guide Overlay** in preview builds showing SMPTE `93%/90%` boxes (for `16:9`) or the `850×1170` Universal Vertical Safe Box + `4:5` Grid Crop (for `9:16`), and strip the overlay automatically in the final production render pass.
     Cultural notes: While `16:9` television sets in modern homes rarely overscan by the `10%–20%` of 1970s CRT televisions, [SMPTE ST 2046-1](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fhubfs%2FSMPTE%2520Standards%2520Quarterly%2520Report%2520September%25202024%2520%281%29.pdf%3FhsLang%3Den) `90%` Title Safe remains universally enforced by broadcast QC because resting typography `< 5%` from the physical monitor bezel creates uncomfortable visual edge tension.
     Failure modes: Uploading a `4K 60fps` `80 Mbps` file to TikTok or Instagram Reels → the platform's aggressive mobile ingestion transcoder crushes the file below `4 Mbps`, introducing worse macroblocking than uploading a pre-optimized `1080×1920 30fps` `14 Mbps` file; placing a hero title at `Y = 1650 px` on a `1080×1920` Reel → covered by the caption text and cropped out of the profile grid.
     Checklist: [ ] `16:9` masters keep action inside `93%` SMPTE ST 2046-1 Action Safe and titles inside `90%` Title Safe [ ] `9:16` vertical masters keep essential text/subjects inside `X ∈ [6.5%, 85.2%]`, `Y ∈ [13.0%, 74.0%]` [ ] `9:16` cover/thumbnail frame checked against center `4:5` (`1080×1350`) and `1:1` (`1080×1080`) crops [ ] TikTok/Reels delivered at native `1080×1920` (`10–18 Mbps`); YouTube delivered at `4K` or high-bitrate `1080p`
     Sources: [SMPTE ST 2046-1 Safe Areas Specification](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fhubfs%2FSMPTE%2520Standards%2520Quarterly%2520Report%2520September%25202024%2520%281%29.pdf%3FhsLang%3Den); [Netflix Partner Help Center](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fstudiopartner.netflix.net%2Fstudio%2Fdelivery-specifications-and-requirements-external); [YouTube Recommended Upload Encoding Settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en); [TikTok In-Feed Ad Specifications](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fads.tiktok.com%2Fresources%2Fhelp%2Farticle%2Ftiktok-auction-in-feed-ads%3Flang%3Den); [Meta/Instagram Reels Specifications](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.facebook.com%2Fbusiness%2Fhelp%2F1038071743007909).
