## Production Paradigm & Cross-Section Decision Trees

All specifications in this manual govern a tool-neutral, declarative, event-driven production architecture observed by planner/author users and artist/director users:

| Production Concept         | Canonical Neutral Specification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| :------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Geometric Primitives**   | `planar element` (`billboard` / `card`), triangulated or quad `mesh`, parametric `curve` (`spline`).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **2D Coordinate Space**    | Special case of 3D space where elements reside on the `+X/+Y` plane (`Z = 0`) and the camera looks down the `−Z` axis (orthographic or narrow-FOV perspective).                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Temporal Architecture**  | Discrete timed events parameterized by `(start, duration, intensity, channel, easing)` following the note-event paradigm of the [MIDI 1.0 Detailed Specification](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmidi.org%2Fmidi-1-0-detailed-specification).                                                                                                            |
| **Authoring Verbs**        | `event`, `cue`, `trigger`, `channel`, `sequence` — replacing manual per-frame curve handle dragging with declarative event blocks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Scene Representation**   | Static, declarative, exchangeable scene description + immutable asset workspace, conceptually aligned with [OpenUSD](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fopenusd.org%2Frelease%2Fintro.html) and [Khronos glTF 2.0](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2FKhronosGroup%2FglTF%2Fblob%2Fmain%2Fspecification%2F2.0%2FSpecification.adoc). |
| **Effects Pipeline**       | Composable, deterministic, shader-based post-processing DAG executed in scene-linear color space.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Rendering Architecture** | GPU rasterization pipeline ([Khronos Vulkan Specification](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.vulkan.org%2Fspec%2Flatest%2Findex.html)); zero runtime ray tracing or path tracing.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Precomputation (Bake)**  | Offline precomputation of heavy lighting, physics simulations, and cloth/fluid dynamics into static textures, lightmaps, spherical harmonics probes, or geometry caches.                                                                                                                                                                                                |

### Master Decision Tree 1: Selecting a Palette & Harmony Architecture

1. **Is the primary communication goal high-urgency warning, binary conflict, or maximum visual salience on a crowded feed?**
   - **Yes** → Select **Complementary Harmony** (`Δh = 180° ± 10°` in [OKLCH](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fbottosson.github.io%2Fposts%2Foklab%2F)) at a strict **85:15 area ratio** (dominant neutral/desaturated field vs. high-chroma accent) to prevent simultaneous-contrast edge vibration ([Itten, The Art of Color](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.goodreads.com%2Fen%2Fbook%2Fshow%2F967310.The_Art_of_Color)).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 2.
2. **Does the scene require calm cohesion, luxury editorial restraint, or data hierarchy without hue distraction?**
   - **Yes** → Select **Monochromatic** (`Δh = 0°`, varying Lightness `L` across `0.15–0.92` and Chroma `C` across `0.02–0.18`) or **Analogous Harmony** (`3 hues` separated by `Δh = 25°–40°`), enforcing a **60:30:10 area distribution** ([Block, The Visual Story](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.routledge.com%2FThe-Visual-Story-Creating-the-Visual-Structure-of-Film-TV-and-Digital-Media%2FBlock%2Fp%2Fbook%2F9781138014152)).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 3.
3. **Does the scene need high-energy contrast while avoiding the harshness of a direct 180° complement?**
   - **Yes** → Select **Split-Complementary Harmony** (base hue `h₀` + two hues at `h₀ + 150°` and `h₀ + 210°` in OKLCH) with area weights **65% base : 25% secondary : 10% accent**.
   - **No** → For multi-character or playful categorical differentiation, use **Triadic Harmony** (`Δh = 120°`, keeping 2 hues desaturated `C ≤ 0.08` and 1 hue saturated `C ≥ 0.16`) or **Tetradic/Rectangular Harmony** (`Δh = 60°, 180°, 240°`, capping maximum simultaneous high-chroma hues at 2 per frame).

### Master Decision Tree 2: Selecting an Easing Family for an Event

1. **Is the property continuous and constant-velocity (e.g., clock sweep, seamless background pan, UV scroll, turntable rotation)?**
   - **Yes** → Use **Linear** (`cubic-bezier(0.0, 0.0, 1.0, 1.0)`).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 2.
2. **Is the element entering the frame from off-screen or materializing from an impulse/impact?**
   - **Yes** → Use **Ease-Out (Deceleration)**:
     - Subtle UI/typography settle: **Cubic Out** (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1.0, 0.36, 1.0)`, `duration = 250–400 ms`).
     - High-energy kinetic snap: **Exponential Out** (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1.0, 0.3, 1.0)`, `duration = 350–600 ms`).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 3.
3. **Is the element exiting the frame permanently or accelerating into a cut?**
   - **Yes** → Use **Ease-In (Acceleration)**: **Quadratic In** (`cubic-bezier(0.55, 0.085, 0.68, 0.53)`) or **Cubic In** (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.0, 0.67, 0.0)`, `duration = 150–300 ms`).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 4.
4. **Is the element repositioning from Point A to Point B while remaining visible on screen?**
   - **Yes** → Does it require organic physical mass and overshoot?
     - **No (Clean editorial/corporate/Swiss style)** → Use **Asymmetric Cubic In-Out** (`cubic-bezier(0.65, 0.0, 0.35, 1.0)` or faster acceleration/longer deceleration `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.0, 0.2, 1.0)`, `duration = 350–700 ms`).
     - **Yes (Tactile/characterful/playful)** → Use **Damped Spring** (`ζ = 0.65–0.82`, natural frequency `ωₙ = 12–22 rad/s` for 1–2 subtle overshoots) or **Back In-Out** (`overshoot s = 1.2–1.7`, peaking at `4–8%` beyond target). Reserve **Bounce** (`restitution e = 0.45–0.65`) strictly for rigid gravity impacts, and **Elastic** (`ζ = 0.25–0.45`) strictly for stylized cartoon rubber/string mechanics ([Williams, The Animator's Survival Kit](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FThe_Animator%2527s_Survival_Kit)).

### Master Decision Tree 3: Selecting an Editorial Cut or Transition

1. **Are the two shots part of the same continuous scene, time, and location?**
   - **Yes** → Is the subject performing a physical movement across the shot boundary?
     - **Yes** → Execute a **Cut on Action** (`2–3 frames` before the peak velocity or impact in Shot A to the continuation in Shot B, with camera angle change `≥ 30°`) ([Murch, In the Blink of an Eye](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FIn_the_Blink_of_an_Eye_%28Murch_book%29); [Mascelli, The Five C's of Cinematography](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.silmanjamespress.com%2Fshop%2Ffilmmaking-directing%2Fthe-five-cs-of-cinematography%2F)).
     - **No** → Is dialogue or voiceover driving the transition?
       - **Yes** → Use an **L-Cut** (audio trails `12–36 frames` into the reaction shot) or **J-Cut** (incoming speaker's audio leads `6–18 frames` before the video cut).
       - **No** → Use a standard **Hard Cut** (0-frame transition).
   - **No (Changing time, space, or conceptual state)** → Proceed to Step 2.
2. **Is the change of state abrupt and intended to shock, awaken, or contrast emotionally?**
   - **Yes** → Use a **Smash Cut** (`0 frames`, pairing a high-contrast `≥ 12 LU` audio transient drop or spike with an immediate visual luminance/scale inversion).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 3.
3. **Is there a strong geometric form, motion vector, or silhouette shared between Outgoing Shot A and Incoming Shot B?**
   - **Yes** → Use a **Graphic Match Cut** (0-frame cut or `4–8 frame` cross-dissolve where focal object screen coordinates match within `≤ 3%` of frame width and velocity vectors align within `±15°`).
   - **No** → Proceed to Step 4.
4. **Does the transition represent a soft passage of time (`hours` to `years`) or contemplative emotional blending?**
   - **Yes** → Use a **Dissolve** (`24–72 frames` at `24 fps`, interpolated in linear light to prevent mid-point darkening).
   - **No (Motion-graphics sequence transitions: Wipe, Push, Whip, Zoom, Luma, Mask Reveal)** → Use **Mask Reveal / Wipe** (`12–24 frames`) when foreground geometry naturally traverses the frame, or **Whip/Slide/Push** (`6–14 frames` with directional motion blur `180°–360°` shutter equivalent) only when motivated by existing camera momentum. Never chain more than 2 stylized motion transitions consecutively `[OPINION]`.

## Cross-Scope Evaluation Matrix Summary (§3 Scope × §4 Axes)

| :------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Pre-production**            | Regional high-context mood vs. direct-response hook norms; Swiss/Bauhaus/anime style anchors.                   | Locks valence/arousal trajectory and vocal breathing room before scene build.                 | Budgets WPM (`130–150`) and CPS (`≤ 15`) to prevent dual-coding overload.                       | Eliminates mid-project style drift and rushed voiceover pacing.                                                | Enforces a single `≤ 15-word` proposition and grayscale thumbnail check.                                | Static style frames + animatic catch `90%` of flaws at `< 5%` of full render cost.                                         |
| **2. 2D Composition**            | Swiss modular grids (`12×8`) vs. East Asian _ma_ negative space vs. variety _telop_ density.                    | Symmetry = monumental authority; asymmetry + negative space (`≥ 35%`) = tension/poise.        | Places anchors at `1/3` or `1/φ` nodes; keeps gaze jump `≤ 15% W` across cuts.                  | Eliminates `2–8 px` near-miss alignments, frame-edge tangents, and UI-clipped text.                            | 3-level scale hierarchy ($\ge 1.414:1$) and macro/micro whitespace ($\ge 2.5:1$).                       | Parametric grid coordinates adapt automatically across aspect ratios.                                                      |
| **3. Typography**                | Neo-Grotesque objectivity vs. Didone fashion vs. CJK weight matching.                                           | Type morphology sets instant tone; kinetic prosody mirrors vocal emotion.                     | Masked baseline reveals + word/line staggers (`2–6f`) pace eye movement.                        | Eliminates proportional-numeral counter jitter (`tnum`), hairline shimmer, and `0 em` display tracking.        | Enforces cap height `≥ 2.2% H`, inverse tracking, and stationary reading hold formula.                  | MSDF fragment shaders keep glyphs sharp at any zoom at minimal VRAM cost.                                                  |
| **4. | **7. Animation Fundamentals**    | Western fluid `1s` (`24 fps`) arcs/overlap vs. Japanese limited `2s/3s` holds + smear frames.                   | Volume-preserving squash/stretch, anticipation (`4–10f`), and damped springs impart life.     | High initial velocity (`expo-out`) captures peripheral vision on Frame 1.                       | Eliminates robotic linear/symmetrical `ease-in-out`, "twinning", and frozen dead holds.                        | Long deceleration tails keep elements readable during the final `40%` of transit.                       | Closed-form spring/Bézier shaders and `≤ 15f` stagger functions replace hand keyframing.                                   |
| **8. 3D Composition**            | Deep-focus cinema (_Welles_) vs. macro shallow-DoF product films vs. miniature clay dioramas.                   | Key-to-Fill ratio (`2:1` high-key vs. `8:1+` low-key) and depth planes sculpt mood.           | Sharp midground + DoF blur + atmospheric haze isolate the hero Z-plane.                         | Eliminates flat frontal flash lighting, double fill shadows, and "floating object" missing AO.                 | Rim lighting (`135°–165°`) separates dark silhouettes from dark backgrounds.                            | Baked directional `UV1` lightmaps (`16–32 texels/m`) + L2 SH probes replace runtime GI.                                    |
| **9.
