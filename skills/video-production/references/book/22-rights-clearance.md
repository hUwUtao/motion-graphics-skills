## 22. Legal & Rights Clearance

### 22.1 Licensing Verification: Music, Fonts, Stock Footage, 3D Assets, and Likeness Rights

Definition: The chain-of-title verification and license-scope audit governing all third-party intellectual property (music composition/master rights, typeface broadcast/embedding EULAs, stock video/textures, 3D meshes, trademarks, and human vocal/visual likenesses) incorporated into a production.
Why:

- Emotion: N/A — Legal and rights clearance operates as a binary institutional compliance layer rather than an affective viewer cue.
- Attention: N/A — Rights metadata is invisible on screen (except mandatory end-credit attributions when required by license).
- Production value: Prevents visible watermarks, unlicensed font substitutions, or sudden post-release copyright takedowns/mutes that destroy a campaign's public launch.
- Clarity: Provides an auditable manifest of territory, duration, media channel (organic web vs. paid broadcast/ad spend), and seat/impression caps for every external asset.
- Cost: Auditing license scope at `Gate 1 (Pre-Production)` prevents catastrophic mid-campaign legal injunctions, Content-ID Demonetization/Muting, or emergency re-scoring and re-rendering.
  Rules:

1. **Music Synchronization + Master Recording Clearance**:
   - Every musical track requires clearance of **two distinct copyrights**: (a) the **Musical Composition (Publishing / Sync License)** from the songwriter/publisher, and (b) the **Master Sound Recording (Master Use License)** from the label/recording artist (or a unified Royalty-Free / Direct-License agreement covering both).
   - Verify that the license explicitly covers the **Distribution Tier**: _Standard Creator / Organic Social_ licenses routinely forbid **Paid Advertising Spend (Boosted Posts / Pre-Roll / Broadcast TV / OTT)** or theatrical distribution above specific impression caps. Log the **Cue Sheet** (Track Title, Composer, Publisher, PRO affiliation `ASCAP/BMI/PRS`, and exact duration `MM:SS`) and clear platform **Content-ID Allowlisting** before launch `[OPINION]`.
2. **Font & Typeface EULA Verification (Desktop vs. Broadcast vs. App Embedding)**:
   - Audit every typeface file (`.otf`, `.ttf`, `.woff2`) against its specific usage model:
     - **Rendered Raster Video (`pixels baked into .mp4`)**: Most standard commercial Desktop EULAs permit rendering static/animated outlines into rasterized video frames, **EXCEPT** many foundry EULAs require a separate **Broadcast / Commercial Advertising Rider** when used in television commercials, paid video ads, or theatrical titles, or forbid using a font as a primary registered **Wordmark Logo** without a Logo License.
     - **Declarative / Interactive Vector Delivery (`.json` Lottie, WebGL, HTML, App UI)**: Embedding the actual `.ttf`/`.otf`/`.woff2` font binary inside a distributable package requires an explicit **App / Web / Digital Ads Embedding License** (or converting short static display titles to raw Bézier spline outlines so no font binary is distributed) ([Lottie v1.0 Specification](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Flottie.github.io%2Flottie-spec%2F1.0%2F)).
     - **Open-Source Safe Fallback**: Open Font License (**SIL OFL 1.1**) typefaces permit unrestricted commercial raster broadcast, modification, and binary embedding without impression caps.
3. **3D Models, Textures, HDRIs, and Stock Footage**:
   - Verify that no 3D mesh, PBR texture scan, or stock plate carries an **"Editorial Use Only"** restriction (which strictly prohibits commercial promotion or advertising).
   - Never redistribute raw, un-encrypted third-party 3D meshes or textures inside an open, downloadable scene archive if the asset license permits _rendered output only_ (bake or lock assets per vendor EULA).
   - Audit background 3D/2D environments for **Incidental Trademarks, Trade Dress, and Copyrighted Art** (e.g., recognizable third-party brand logos on props, copyrighted architectural landmarks requiring commercial property releases, or recognizable vehicle trade dress): remove or replace all third-party logos with neutral geometric designs before baking textures.
4. **Human Likeness, Voice, and Synthetic/AI Provenance**:
   - Secure signed **Talent Model & Vocal Releases** for every recognizable human face, silhouette, or voice recording, explicitly specifying territory (`Worldwide`), term (`Perpetual` or `N years`), and media (`Organic + Paid Digital/Broadcast`).
   - Never clone, synthesize, or imitate a real person's voice or facial likeness without explicit written contractual consent; maintain a complete **Asset Provenance Manifest** logging the origin and license URI of every asset in the workspace.
     Event mapping: Scene root compliance manifest `legal_provenance_manifest(assets=[{asset_id, type: "music"|"font"|"mesh"|"voice", license_tier: "commercial_paid_ads"|"sil_ofl"|"cc0", territory: "worldwide", expiration_date: "perpetual", cue_sheet_logged: true}])`.
     Raster/bake note: When delivering declarative vector scenes (such as [Lottie JSON](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Flottie.github.io%2Flottie-spec%2F1.0%2F)) where font binary embedding rights are unavailable, convert text layers to baked Bézier path geometry (`chars_to_splines = true`) during export compilation.
     Cultural notes: Intellectual property rules on architectural landmarks vary by jurisdiction: for example, while daytime views of the Eiffel Tower are public domain, its nighttime illuminated lighting design is protected under French copyright law and requires clearance for commercial video; European GDPR and US state right-of-publicity statutes strictly govern biometric voice and facial likeness.
     Failure modes: Using an "Organic Social Only" music track or "Free for Personal Use" font in a paid commercial video campaign → automatic platform audio muting, DMCA takedown, and retroactive statutory copyright damages; leaving a recognizable third-party brand logo on a downloaded 3D prop mesh → client legal rejection at final delivery.
     Checklist: [ ] Music cleared for both Sync (Composition) and Master rights matching Paid/Broadcast/Organic tier [ ] Music Cue Sheet and Content-ID allowlist logged [ ] Font EULAs verified for Broadcast/Commercial video and/or binary vector embedding (or SIL OFL 1.1 used) [ ] All 3D meshes, textures, and stock plates cleared for Commercial Use (zero "Editorial Only" assets or incidental third-party trademarks) [ ] Signed Talent/Voice Releases and complete Asset Provenance Manifest archived
     Sources: [Lottie v1.0 Specification](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Flottie.github.io%2Flottie-spec%2F1.0%2F); [Netflix Delivery Specifications \& Requirements](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fstudiopartner.netflix.net%2Fstudio%2Fdelivery-specifications-and-requirements-external).
