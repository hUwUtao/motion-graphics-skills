## 23. Glossary of Production & Pipeline Terms

| Canonical Term                                       | Definition in This Manual                                                                                                                                                      | Cross-Industry Synonyms (Film / 3D / Motion / Game / Audio)                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :--------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **180-Degree Rule**                                  | Spatial continuity constraint keeping all cameras on one side of the primary axis of action between subjects.                                                                  | Line of Action, Axis of Action, Director's Line, Screen Direction Grammar ([Mascelli](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.silmanjamespress.com%2Fshop%2Ffilmmaking-directing%2Fthe-five-cs-of-cinematography%2F))                                |
| **30-Degree Rule**                                   | Rule requiring consecutive shots of the same subject to differ by $\ge 30^\circ$ camera angle and/or $\ge 35\%$ scale.                                                         | Angle-Plus-Distance Rule, Anti-Jump-Cut Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **ACES / ACEScg**                                    | Academy Color Encoding System; `ACEScg` is the scene-linear AP1-primaries working space for CG rendering and compositing.                                                      | Scene-Linear Wide Gamut, AP1 Linear, OCIO Working Space ([ACES](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.acescentral.com%2F))                                                                                                                        |
| **Action Safe / Title Safe**                         | Protective interior raster margins (`93%` Action Safe, `90%` Title Safe per SMPTE ST 2046-1) plus platform UI exclusion zones.                                                 | Safe Area, Overscan Margin, UI Safe Zone, Essential Graphics Area ([SMPTE ST 2046-1](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.smpte.org%2Fhubfs%2FSMPTE%2520Standards%2520Quarterly%2520Report%2520September%25202024%2520%281%29.pdf%3FhsLang%3Den)) |
| **Albedo (Base Color)**                              | Intrinsic diffuse reflectance ratio of a dielectric surface (`[0.03, 0.85]` linear) or $F_0$ specular tint of a conductor.                                                     | Diffuse Color, Base Color, Surface Reflectance ([Burley](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdisneyanimation.com%2Fpublications%2Fphysically-based-shading-at-disney%2F))                                                                            |
| **Ambient Occlusion (AO)**                           | Ratio of non-occluded hemisphere above a surface point, applied strictly to indirect diffuse/specular light.                                                                   | Crevice Shadow, Cavity Map, GTAO, HBAO+, Dirt Map                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Animatic**                                         | Frame-accurate sequence of storyboard panels or coarse 3D playblasts timed to scratch voiceover and music beat grid.                                                           | Leica Reel, Story Reel, Previs Cut, Timed Board                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Anticipation**                                     | Pre-action counter-movement (`8%–18%` opposite travel over `4–10 frames`) preparing the viewer's eye for a primary launch.                                                     | Wind-Up, Pre-Action Cue, Take                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **AOV (Arbitrary Output Variable)**                  | Isolated shading component buffer (Diffuse, Specular, Emission, Z-Depth, Velocity, Cryptomatte) output by the renderer.                                                        | Render Pass, Render Element, MRT Attachment, G-Buffer Channel                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Associated Alpha (Premultiplied)**                 | Alpha representation where RGB channels are pre-scaled by coverage $\alpha$, enabling unified Porter-Duff `Over` compositing of opaque, translucent, and additive glow pixels. | Premultiplied Alpha (vs. Unassociated / Straight Alpha)                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Average Shot Length (ASL)**                        | Mean duration of shots within a sequence or act ($\mu_{\text{ASL}}$), paired with Coefficient of Variation ($\sigma / \mu$).                                                   | Cut Rate, Editorial Pace, Shot Rhythm                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Bake**                                             | Offline precomputation of expensive simulations, global illumination, or procedural deformations into static textures or caches.                                               | Cache, Precompute, Lightmap Bake, Geometry Freeze, Point Cache                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Channel**                                          | Addressable numeric property of an element (`pos_x`, `rot_z`, `scale`, `opacity`, `oklch_C`, `morph_weight`) driven by timed events.                                           | Track, Parameter, Animatable Property, Attribute, Curve                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Chroma Subsampling (`4:2:0` / `4:2:2` / `4:4:4`)** | Encoding scheme reducing color difference (`Cb/Cr`) spatial resolution relative to luminance (`Y'`).                                                                           | YUV Subsampling, YCbCr Format, Color Resolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Circle of Confusion (CoC)**                        | Optical diameter (in mm or screen pixels) of an out-of-focus point source on the image sensor.                                                                                 | Defocus Radius, Bokeh Blur Size, Depth-of-Field Kernel                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Color Script**                                     | Horizontal timeline map plotting the progression of scene palettes, lightness, and contrast across the entire narrative arc.                                                   | Visual Score, Palette Timeline, Lighting Continuity Board                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Contrapposto**                                     | Asymmetric weight-bearing pose where pelvis and shoulder axes counter-tilt in opposite directions ($\pm 5^\circ\text{–}12^\circ$).                                             | Weight Shift, Counter-Balance Pose, Hip-Shoulder Tilt                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Curve (Spline)**                                   | Parametric 1D path primitive in 2D/3D space defined by control knots and basis functions (Bézier, B-spline, NURBS).                                                            | Path, Vector Stroke, Beziér Spline, Rail                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Cut on Action**                                    | Editorial transition cutting `2–3 frames` before the apex of a physical movement in Shot A to its continuation in Shot B.                                                      | Match-on-Action, Movement Edit, Continuity Cut                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **dBTP (Decibels True Peak)**                        | Peak audio signal level measured with $\ge 4\times$ oversampling to capture inter-sample analog reconstruction excursions.                                                     | True Peak Level, Inter-Sample Peak ([ITU-R BS.1770](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2FR-REC-BS.1770))                                                                                                                          |
| **Display-Referred**                                 | Color data bounded to `[0.0, 1.0]` encoded with an inverse EOTF (sRGB, BT.1886, PQ) for direct emission by a target monitor.                                                   | Output-Referred, Gamma-Encoded, SDR/HDR Display Space                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Dolly / Truck / Crane**                            | Physical translation of the camera in local `-Z/+Z` (Dolly), `±X` (Truck), or `±Y` (Crane/Pedestal), generating 3D motion parallax.                                            | Camera Track, Boom/Jib, Push-In / Pull-Back                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Easing Family**                                    | Mathematical transfer function mapping normalized event time $t \in [0, 1]$ to spatial progress (Quad, Cubic, Expo, Back, Spring).                                             | Interpolation Curve, Velocity Profile, Slow-In/Slow-Out, F-Curve                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Effector (Spatial Field)**                         | Volumetric or parametric falloff function modulating transforms/colors across an instanced ensemble based on world position.                                                   | Falloff Field, Mograph Effector, Attribute Mask, Force Field                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Event / Cue / Trigger**                            | Discrete declarative timing block `(start, duration, intensity, channel, easing)` that mutates scene state without manual keyframing.                                          | Note Event ([MIDI](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fmidi.org%2Fmidi-1-0-detailed-specification)), Action Clip, Tween, State Transition                                                                                                            |
| **Halation**                                         | Warm red-orange glow (`OKLCH h ≈ 32°`) around high-contrast bright edges caused by light back-scattering through film emulsion.                                                | Emulsion Scatter, Red Edge Glow                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Hit Point (Synchresis)**                           | Frame-accurate alignment (`0f` to `-2f` visual lead) of a visual cut or kinetic accent with an audio transient.                                                                | Audio-Visual Sync, Beat Hit, Accent Point ([Chion](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fbooks.google.com%2Fbooks%2Fabout%2FAudio_vision.html%3Fid%3Dis_HyxAyMkMC))                                                                                    |
| **J-Cut / L-Cut (Split Edit)**                       | Editorial cut where incoming audio leads incoming video by `6–18f` (J-Cut) or outgoing audio trails into incoming video by `12–36f` (L-Cut).                                   | Split Edit, Audio Lead / Audio Overlap                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Light Probe (SH / Cubemap)**                       | Spatial sample storing diffuse irradiance (Order-2 Spherical Harmonics) or pre-convolved glossy reflections (Cubemap) for dynamic meshes.                                      | Irradiance Volume, Reflection Capture, Environment Probe                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **LUFS / LKFS**                                      | Loudness Units (or K-weighted Loudness) Full Scale; perceptual integrated loudness measurement per ITU-R BS.1770 / EBU R128 (`1 LU = 1 dB`).                                   | Integrated Program Loudness ([ITU-R BS.1770](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.itu.int%2Frec%2FR-REC-BS.1770); [EBU R 128](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fen.wikipedia.org%2Fwiki%2FEBU_R_128))                                 |
| **M&E (Music & Effects)**                            | Complete mastered audio stem containing all music, sound design, ambience, and Foley without Dialogue/VO, used for international localization.                                 | International Stem, Minus-Dialogue Mix, Dubbing Master                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **MatCap (Material Capture)**                        | Stylized/fast raster shader mapping view-space surface normals `N_view.xy` directly to a 2D lit sphere texture.                                                                | LitSphere, Normal-Mapped Studio Sphere                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Mesh**                                             | 3D surface primitive composed of vertices, edges, and triangles/quads with associated attributes (`pos`, `normal`, `uv`, `tangent`).                                           | Polygon Geometry, Surface Mesh, SubD Cage                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **MSDF (Multi-Channel Signed Distance Field)**       | Texture representation encoding exact vector corner distances in RGB channels for resolution-independent crisp glyph rasterization.                                            | Distance Field Font, Vector Texture Atlas                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **NCLC Tags**                                        | Container metadata integers (`Primaries`, `Transfer Function`, `Matrix`) telling video players how to decode YCbCr to display RGB (`e.g., 1-1-1`).                             | VUI Color Flags, `colr` Atom, Color Space Metadata                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **OKLab / OKLCH**                                    | Perceptually uniform color space by Björn Ottosson (`L` = Lightness, `C` = Chroma, `h` = Hue angle) free of CIELAB's blue hue warp.                                            | Perceptual Color Space, Cylindrical Oklab ([Ottosson](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fbottosson.github.io%2Fposts%2Foklab%2F))                                                                                                                   |
| **Overlap / Follow-Through**                         | Phase offset (`1–5 frames`) between parent and child joints or between translation, rotation, and scale channels on a single object.                                           | Breaking Joints, Drag, Secondary Lag, Wave Principle                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Parallel Transport Frame**                         | Twist-free coordinate frame propagated along a 3D spline that prevents the $180^\circ$ flipping artifacts of Frenet-Serret frames.                                             | Rotation-Minimizing Frame (RMF), Bishop Frame, Rail-Guided Curve                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Planar Element**                                   | 2D quad/card or vector shape residing on a local `+X/+Y` plane in 3D space (`camera looks -Z`).                                                                                | Billboard, Card, 2D Layer, Sprite, Graphic Plane                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Scene-Referred Linear**                            | Radiometric color space (`[0.0, +∞)`) where values are directly proportional to physical photon energy prior to display tone mapping.                                          | Linear Light, Scene Linear, Unbounded Float                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Shutter Angle**                                    | Temporal exposure window expressed as degrees of a $360^\circ$ rotary shutter ($180^\circ = 50\%$ of frame interval), governing motion blur length.                            | Exposure Duration, Motion Blur Length                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Simultaneous Contrast**                            | Optical illusion where a background field shifts the perceived lightness and hue of an enclosed foreground swatch toward the background's complement.                          | Albers Effect, Induced Contrast ([Albers](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fbooks.google.com%2Fbooks%2Fabout%2FInteraction_of_Color.html%3Fid%3DA_MYU_XDXfcC))                                                                                     |
| **Track Matte (Alpha / Luma)**                       | Independent stencil element whose coverage (`Alpha`) or BT.709 luminance (`Luma`) clips the visibility of another layer.                                                       | Stencil Layer, Mask Layer, Clipping Matte                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **VAT (Vertex Animation Texture)**                   | `16-bit/32-bit float` image texture encoding per-vertex position/normal offsets (`X = vertex_id`, `Y = frame`) for zero-CPU GPU playback of baked cloth/fluids.                | Baked Deformation Texture, Morph Texture Cache ([SideFX](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.sidefx.com%2Fdocs%2F))                                                                                                                              |
| **Viseme**                                           | Visual mouth/jaw/lip pose corresponding to a phonetic group (`M/B/P`, `OO/W`, `F/V`, `AH`), triggered `1–2 frames` ahead of audio.                                             | Lip-Sync Shape, Phoneme Pose, Mouth Morph Target                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
