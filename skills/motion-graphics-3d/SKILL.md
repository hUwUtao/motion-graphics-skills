# Motion Graphics 3D

Stage readable spatial compositions, choose materials and lighting for the intended look, and plan animated or simulated elements around timing and render constraints. The source manual assumes raster rendering and baked heavy simulation; do not treat that assumption as a universal limitation of other tools.
