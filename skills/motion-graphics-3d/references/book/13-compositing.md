## 13. Compositing

### 13.1 Layering, Blend Modes, Alpha Premultiplication, and Render Passes (AOVs)

Definition: The mathematical assembly of multi-layer 2D/3D buffers and Arbitrary Output Variables (`AOVs` / Render Passes) in scene-linear floating-point space governed by Porter-Duff alpha algebra ([ACES Documentation](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.acescentral.com%2F); [Blender Compositing Manual](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.blender.org%2Fmanual%2Fen%2Flatest%2F)).
Why:

- Emotion: Fine-tuning specular, rim, and atmospheric passes in compositing allows instant emotional dial-in without waiting for 3D re-renders.
- Attention: Isolating the hero subject via Cryptomatte / Object-ID masks enables targeted +0.3 EV contrast boosts that lock viewer gaze.
- Production value: Strict **Associated (Premultiplied) Linear Alpha** math eliminates the dark 1-pixel "dirty halo" or bright jagged fringe around semi-transparent edges, motion blur, and glowing particles.
- Clarity: Ensures foreground graphics, 3D renders, and background plates integrate into a unified optical space.
- Cost: Adjusting lighting intensity ratios, color tints, or depth-of-field via multi-pass AOV reconstruction takes `< 1 ms` per frame in the compositor vs. hours of 3D re-baking.
  Rules:

1. **The Associated (Premultiplied) Linear Alpha Law**:
   - Store and composite all rendered layers in **Scene-Linear RGB with Associated (Premultiplied) Alpha** `(R_premul, G_premul, B_premul, α)` where the RGB channels already contain the emitted/reflected radiance scaled by coverage $\alpha$, and execute the canonical **Porter-Duff Over Operation**:
     $$\mathbf{C}_{\text{out}} = \mathbf{C}_{\text{fg}} + \mathbf{C}_{\text{bg}} \cdot (1.0 - \alpha_{\text{fg}}), \quad \alpha_{\text{out}} = \alpha_{\text{fg}} + \alpha_{\text{bg}} \cdot (1.0 - \alpha_{\text{fg}})$$
   - **Luminous Additive Fire/Glow Exception**: Because associated premultiplied alpha decouples RGB emission from occlusion $\alpha$, a pure additive glow or fire element simply sets $\mathbf{C}_{\text{fg}} > (0, 0, 0)$ while keeping $\alpha_{\text{fg}} = 0.0$— allowing a single `Over` operator to composite solid opaque surfaces ($\alpha = 1$), semi-transparent smoke ($0 < \alpha < 1$), and pure additive fire/bloom ($\alpha = 0$) simultaneously without switching blend modes!
   - When applying a color-grade curve, exposure gain, or LUT to a premultiplied layer, **always Unpremultiply first (`RGB / max(α, 1e-5)`), apply the color operation, and Re-Premultiply (`RGB * α`)** so semi-transparent motion-blurred edges do not shift hue or clip ([Blender Manual](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.blender.org%2Fmanual%2Fen%2Flatest%2F); [DaVinci Resolve Manual](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocuments.blackmagicdesign.com%2FUserManuals%2FDaVinci-Resolve-20-Beginners-Guide.pdf%3F_v%3D1757574013000)).
2. **Blend Mode Physics (Ban Display-Space Screen/Overlay on Linear Lights)**:
   - In scene-linear compositing, use **Add (`Plus`: $A + B$)** for physical light phenomena (bloom, light wraps, sparks, reflections) and **Multiply ($A \cdot B$)** for physical absorption/transmission (shadows, vignettes, dirt occlusion).
   - Reserve nonlinear display-space blend modes (**Screen**: $1 - (1-A)(1-B)$, **Overlay**, **Soft Light**) strictly for stylized 2D graphic design layers clamped to `[0.0, 1.0]`; applying `Screen` or `Overlay` to unbounded scene-linear buffers (`RGB > 1.0`) produces negative inversions and broken highlights.
3. **Canonical Beauty AOV Reconstruction Formula**:
   Export `16-bit half-float` (`DWAA/DWAB` or `PIZ` OpenEXR) passes and verify that they reconstruct the Beauty pass to within `< 1e-4` error before applying creative per-pass grades:
   $$\mathbf{C}_{\text{beauty}} = \left(\mathbf{L}_{\text{diffuse\_direct}} + \mathbf{L}_{\text{diffuse\_indirect}} \cdot \text{AO}\right) \cdot \mathbf{A}_{\text{albedo}} + \mathbf{L}_{\text{specular\_direct}} + \mathbf{L}_{\text{specular\_indirect}} \cdot \text{SO} + \mathbf{L}_{\text{transmission}} + \mathbf{L}_{\text{emission}}$$
   Include utility passes (`Z-Depth` in `32-bit float`, `2D Motion Vector` in `16-bit float`, `World Normal` in `16-bit float`, and `Cryptomatte / Object ID` in `32-bit float`) **without anti-aliasing (`box 1×1 filter`)** on `Z-Depth` and `Object ID` (or using deep/coverage pairs) so depth edges do not interpolate false mid-air Z values between foreground and background!
4. **Light Wrap Integration**: When compositing a foreground element over a bright background (`L_bg > 0.65`), bleed a blurred copy of the background (`blur_radius = 1%–4%` of frame width, masked by the inverted foreground edge alpha) onto the foreground perimeter using `Add` in linear space at `8%–20%` intensity so bright sky/light wraps naturally around the silhouette edge ([Birn, Digital Lighting and Rendering](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2F3drender.com%2Flight%2Findex.html)).
   Event mapping: Compositor DAG graph node `comp_layer_node(fg_aov, bg_aov, mode="porter_duff_over"|"linear_add"|"multiply", unpremult_grade=true, light_wrap_radius_pct=2.0, light_wrap_gain=0.12)`.
   Raster/bake note: Output Beauty, Specular, Emission, Z-Depth, Normal, and Velocity AOVs in a single geometry pass via **Multiple Render Targets (MRT)** in the GPU rasterizer (`glFragData[0..5]` / Vulkan subpass color attachments) so extracting AOVs adds zero extra vertex transformation passes.
   Cultural notes: Feature animation and VFX pipelines mandate multi-channel `OpenEXR` ACEScg compositing with Cryptomatte ID mattes; motion graphics pipelines increasingly use real-time GPU MRT compositing graphs directly inside the scene renderer.
   Failure modes: Multiplying an _already premultiplied_ image by its alpha channel a second time ("double premultiplication") → a 1–2 pixel dark black halo rings every anti-aliased edge and motion-blurred trail; anti-aliasing a raw Z-Depth buffer (`Z_fg = 1.0 m`, `Z_bg = 100.0 m`) → the edge pixel gets `Z = 50.5 m`, causing depth-of-field and fog shaders to create a razor-sharp artifact ring around the character.
   Checklist: [ ] All compositing executed in Scene-Linear RGB with Associated (Premultiplied) Alpha [ ] Grade nodes wrapped in `Unpremultiply → Grade → Premultiply` [ ] Physical light additions use `Linear Add (Plus)`, never `Screen`/`Overlay` on values `> 1.0` [ ] `Z-Depth` passed un-filtered (or via min-depth / coverage) to prevent false edge depth interpolation [ ] Subtle `8%–20%` linear Light Wrap applied to foreground silhouettes over bright backgrounds
   Sources: [ACES Documentation](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.acescentral.com%2F); [OpenColorIO Documentation](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fopencolorio.readthedocs.io%2Fprojects%2Fconfig-aces%2Fen%2Flatest%2F); [Birn, Digital Lighting and Rendering](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2F3drender.com%2Flight%2Findex.html); [Blender Compositing Manual](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.blender.org%2Fmanual%2Fen%2Flatest%2F).

### 13.2 Optical Post-Effects (Glow, Bloom, Chromatic Aberration, Grain, Vignette, Lens Distortion, Halation) and Kitsch Thresholds

Definition: The final full-screen shader stack simulating the physical imperfections and light-scattering properties of glass lenses, photochemical film emulsion, and sensor gates—bounded by strict numeric thresholds that separate subtle optical realism from amateur kitsch.
Why:

- Emotion: Subtle halation, film grain, and anamorphic lens breathe warmth, nostalgia, and tactile physical presence into mathematically sterile digital pixels.
- Attention: Natural optical vignette (`-0.25 to -0.60 EV` corner falloff) and localized threshold bloom guide the eye inward toward the brightest focal highlights.
- Production value: Physically grounded post-effects (multi-scale energy-conserving mip-bloom, luminance-weighted grain, radial-only chromatic dispersion) bridge CG and cinema; over-cranked effects are the #1 mark of novice over-processing.
- Clarity: Keeping post-effects strictly below the kitsch ceiling preserves typographic sharpness and color purity.
- Cost: The entire 7-effect optical post-processing chain executes in `< 0.8 ms` at `4K` on a GPU raster pipeline using downsampled mip pyramids and single-pass fragment math.
  Rules:

1. Enforce the **Optical Post-Effect Physical Parameter vs. Kitsch Threshold Matrix**:

| Optical Effect                | Physical Mechanism & Shader Implementation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Crafted Subtle Range (Target)                                                                                         | Kitsch / Amateur Failure Threshold (Forbidden)                                                                                                               |
| :---------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bloom / Glow**              | Scattering inside lens elements & human eye ciliary corona. Implement as a **6–8 mip-level Dual-Filter / Karis Downsample-Upsample Pyramid** in scene-linear space with soft knee threshold $Y \ge 1.0\text{–}1.5$, blended via `mix(scene_linear, bloom_mip, 0.03–0.08)`.                                              | `3%–8%` energy-conserving linear mix; highlights glow softly without washing out mid-tones.                           | `> 15%` additive glow or single-radius Gaussian blur → whole frame looks foggy, radioactive, or smeared with Vaseline `[OPINION]`.                           |
| **Halation (Film Emulsion)**  | Red-wavelength light penetrating film emulsion, bouncing off the anti-halation backing, and scattering back into the red layer around high-contrast edges. Blur highlights ($Y > 1.2$) with radius `0.4%–1.2% W` and tint strictly red-orange (`OKLCH h ≈ 32°, C ≈ 0.22`) at `4%–10%` linear gain.                      | Subtle warm red-orange aura clinging tightly to bright window edges, neon signs, or specular rims.                    | Wide orange/red smear (`> 2.5% W`) bleeding across mid-tone faces and turning white subtitles pink.                                                          |
| **Chromatic Aberration (CA)** | Wavelength-dependent refractive index dispersion near lens periphery. Scale radial UV offset quadratically from frame center: $\Delta \mathbf{uv}_c = k_c \cdot (\mathbf{uv} - 0.5) \cdot \|\mathbf{uv} - 0.5\|^2$ using a **3–7 tap spectral ramp** (`0.0 px` at center; `0.5–1.8 px` max at extreme `1080p` corners). | `0.0 px` in center `60%` of frame; `≤ 1.5 px` soft spectral softness at far corners.                                  | Uniform full-screen horizontal RGB channel shift (`> 3 px`) or red/cyan 3D-glasses fringing across center-screen typography ("TikTok glitch filter kitsch"). |
| **Lens Distortion**           | Barrel ($k_1 < 0$, wide lenses) or Pincushion ($k_1 > 0$, telephoto/anamorphic) Brown-Conrady radial warp: $r' = r(1 + k_1 r^2 + k_2 r^4)$.                                                                                                                                                                             | $k_1 \in [-0.04, +0.03]$ (subtle organic barrel on wide 3D shots; **`0.00` on UI/grid typography cards**).            | $                                                                                                                                                            | k_1 | > 0.12$ (unless intentional action-camera/fisheye POV), warping straight typographic grid lines into bowed bananas. |
| **Optical Vignette**          | Natural $\cos^4(\theta)$ illumination falloff across wide lens apertures, applied **in Scene-Linear space before tone mapping**: $L_{\text{out}} = L_{\text{in}} \cdot \cos^4(\theta \cdot v_{\text{strength}})$.                                                                                                       | `-0.25 to -0.65 EV` (`15%–35%` linear light reduction at extreme corners), preserving highlight punch inside corners. | Post-tone-map black-circle overlay (`> 50%` opacity) that turns bright white skies/backgrounds into muddy charcoal gray corners.                             |
| **Film Grain**                | Silver-halide dye clouds scaled by luminance response. Use blue-noise or scanned 35mm grain (`size = 1.2–1.8 px` at `1080p`), modulated by luminance weight $w(Y) = 4 Y (1 - Y)$ so grain peaks in mid-tones/shadows and vanishes to `0%` in clipped whites (`RMS = 1.0%–2.5%`).                                        | Fine, organic 35mm texture (`1.0%–2.5%` RMS) that also prevents 8-bit gradient banding.                               | Static un-animated noise overlay, huge `4 px` blocky monochrome snow (`> 5%` RMS), or heavy grain inside `100%` specular whites.                             |

2. **Execution Order in the Post-Process Stack**:
   Always execute optical post-effects in the exact physical order light travels through a camera:
   $$\text{Scene Linear Render} \rightarrow \text{DoF} \rightarrow \text{Motion Blur} \rightarrow \text{Optical Vignette }\left(\cos^4\theta\right) \rightarrow \text{Lens Distortion + Radial CA} \rightarrow \text{Multi-Mip Bloom} \rightarrow \text{Halation} \rightarrow \text{Tone Mapping (RRT+ODT)} \rightarrow \text{Luminance-Weighted Grain + Blue-Noise Dither}$$
   Event mapping: `post_process_stack_descriptor(vignette_ev=-0.40, lens_k1=-0.015, ca_corner_max_px=1.2, bloom_threshold=1.2, bloom_mix=0.05, halation_gain=0.06, grain_rms=0.015)`.
   Raster/bake note: Use a single Karis-weighted `13-tap` downsample and `9-tap` tent upsample filter chain (`6 mip levels`) in `R11G11B10_UFLOAT` for Bloom—Karis weighting suppresses single-pixel sub-pixel fireflies during the first downsample step so specular highlights never explode into flickering star crosses.
   Cultural notes: Clean Swiss/Tech UI motion graphics frequently disable Lens Distortion, CA, and Halation completely (`0.0`), retaining only `3%` linear Bloom and `1.0%` anti-banding Dither/Grain; nostalgic/analog-inspired title design and music videos push Halation (`8%`) and 16mm Grain (`3.0%` RMS) as primary aesthetic textures `[OPINION]`.
   Failure modes: Applying a black-oval vignette _after_ the sRGB Output Transform → bright clouds or white backgrounds in the corners turn a dirty, unnatural gray instead of simply reducing exposure by `0.4 EV`; applying Chromatic Aberration uniformly across the center of the frame → makes small body text blurry and induces eye strain.
   Checklist: [ ] Post-process stack follows physical optical order (Linear effects → Tone Map → Grain/Dither) [ ] Bloom uses `6–8 mip` energy-conserving linear pyramid (`3%–8%` mix) with Karis firefly suppression [ ] Chromatic Aberration is strictly radial (`0 px` at center, `≤ 1.5 px` at corners) [ ] Vignette applied in linear exposure space (`-0.25 to -0.65 EV` via $\cos^4\theta$) before tone mapping [ ] Grain weighted by luminance $4Y(1-Y)$ at `1.0%–2.5%` RMS
   Sources: [Brown, Cinematography: Theory and Practice](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.routledge.com%2FCinematography-Theory-and-Practice-For-Cinematographers-and-Directors%2FBrown%2Fp%2Fbook%2F9780367373450); [ACES Documentation](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocs.acescentral.com%2F); [DaVinci Resolve Reference Manual](https://www.google.com/search?q=https%3A%2F%2Fdocuments.blackmagicdesign.com%2FUserManuals%2FDaVinci-Resolve-20-Beginners-Guide.pdf%3F_v%3D1757574013000).
